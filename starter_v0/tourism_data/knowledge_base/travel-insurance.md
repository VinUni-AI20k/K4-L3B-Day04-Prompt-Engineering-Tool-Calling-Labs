# Travel insurance

Travel insurance is optional but strongly recommended for international tourists.

## Partners

- **PVI Insurance**: standard plan 150,000 VND/trip, premium plan 350,000 VND/trip.
- **Bao Viet Travel**: family plan covers up to 6 members, 800,000 VND.
- **Liberty Insurance**: annual multi-trip plan for frequent travellers.

## Coverage

- Medical emergencies up to 1 billion VND.
- Trip cancellation due to covered reasons.
- Lost luggage up to 50 million VND per item.

## How to buy

- Add insurance during the booking flow.
- The insurance certificate is emailed within 24 hours.

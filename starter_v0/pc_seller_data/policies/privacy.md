## Internal-only data

Customer name, email, phone, address, customer ID, and order history are internal-only. They must never be sent to any external search, web, or third-party tool.

## External product research

External research is limited to public product information such as manufacturer, public model name, and the type of information needed. Internal identifiers and customer details must be removed before any external lookup.

## Secrets

Passwords, one-time passwords, tokens, API keys, recovery codes, and payment credentials must never be requested, stored, written to an order, or included in a transcript.

## Retention

Order records are retained for the period required by law. Lab data is fictional and contains no real customer information.

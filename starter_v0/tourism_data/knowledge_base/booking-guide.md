# Booking flow

Follow these steps to book a room or package:

1. Search by destination and travel dates.
2. Compare hotels, packages, and prices.
3. Select a room type and number of guests.
4. Provide guest details (full name as on passport, contact email, phone).
5. Choose a payment method.
6. Confirm the booking; you will receive a booking ID in the format BK-XXXX.

## Group bookings (more than 4 guests)

- Contact our group sales team directly.
- A dedicated coordinator will help with itinerary and discount.

## Special requests

- Late check-in, dietary requirements, accessibility: add them in the "special requests" field during booking.
- The hotel will try to accommodate but cannot guarantee all requests.

# Payment methods

We accept the following payment methods for bookings:

## Bank transfer (Vietnam)

- Vietcombank, BIDV, Techcombank, MB Bank.
- Use the booking ID (BK-XXXX) as the transfer reference.
- Bookings are auto-confirmed within 30 minutes of receipt.

## Credit and debit cards

- Visa, Mastercard, JCB.
- 3D Secure verification required.
- Installment plans (3, 6, 12 months) available for cards issued by Vietcombank, Techcombank, and Sacombank.

## E-wallets

- MoMo, ZaloPay, ShopeePay for bookings up to 20 million VND.

## On-site payment

- Available at Saigon Sky Hotel only.
- Cash or card accepted at check-in.

> Payment confirmation triggers automatic booking confirmation. Always verify the booking ID before confirming.

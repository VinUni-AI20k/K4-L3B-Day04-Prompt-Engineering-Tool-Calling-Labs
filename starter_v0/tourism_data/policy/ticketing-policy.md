# Ticketing policy

## Priority levels

| Priority | First response | Resolution target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| critical | 30 minutes | 4 hours |
| high | 2 hours | 1 business day |
| medium | 8 hours | 3 business days |
| low | 1 business day | 7 business days |

## When to create a ticket

- Booking or payment issue that cannot be resolved in chat.
- Refund request exceeding the auto-approval threshold (5 million VND).
- Complaints about hotel service quality.

## Sensitive data in tickets

- Never include passwords, OTPs, MFA codes, full credit card numbers.
- Mask customer phone numbers and emails before forwarding to hotels.

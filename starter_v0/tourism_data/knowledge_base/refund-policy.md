# Refund and cancellation policy

## Free cancellation

- Cancel at least 7 days before check-in: 100% refund.
- Cancel 3-6 days before check-in: 70% refund.
- Cancel within 48 hours of check-in: 50% refund.

## Non-refundable bookings

- Some promotional rates and peak-season packages are non-refundable.
- The booking confirmation email states the cancellation terms for the specific package.

## How to request a refund

1. Contact customer support with your booking ID (format BK-XXXX).
2. Provide a reason and preferred refund method (bank transfer or original payment).
3. Refunds are processed within 7-10 working days.

## Force majeure

- For natural disasters, government travel restrictions, or airline cancellations, full refunds are issued regardless of the original cancellation window.

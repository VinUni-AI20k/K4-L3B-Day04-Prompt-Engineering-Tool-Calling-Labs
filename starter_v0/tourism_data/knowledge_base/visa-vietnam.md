# Vietnam visa requirements

Most foreign tourists need a visa to enter Vietnam unless they come from a visa-exempt country.

## E-visa

- Available for citizens of 80+ countries.
- Single entry or multiple entry, up to 90 days.
- Apply online at the official immigration portal; processing time 3 working days.

## Visa on arrival

- Requires a pre-approved letter from a Vietnamese sponsor.
- Pay stamping fee at the airport (USD 25 single / USD 50 multi).

## Visa exemption

- Citizens of ASEAN countries, Japan, South Korea, and several European nations can stay 14-45 days without a visa.
- Check the latest list on the immigration department website before travel.

## Required documents

- Passport valid for at least 6 months beyond the entry date.
- Two recent passport photos (for visa on arrival).
- Confirmed return ticket or onward travel.

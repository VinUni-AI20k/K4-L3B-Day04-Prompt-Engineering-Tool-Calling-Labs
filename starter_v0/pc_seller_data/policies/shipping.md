## Delivery options

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs an extra 25 USD. Custom builds add 2 business days for assembly and burn-in testing.

## Tracking

A tracking number is issued when the order leaves the warehouse. Prebuilt systems ship assembled and tested.

## Delivery changes

The delivery option can be changed until the order is packed. After packing, only the carrier can redirect the parcel.

## Failed delivery

After two failed delivery attempts the parcel returns to the warehouse and the customer is contacted to arrange a new shipment.

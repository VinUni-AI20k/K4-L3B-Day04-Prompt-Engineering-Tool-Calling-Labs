## Accepted methods

The store accepts major credit and debit cards, bank transfer, and approved installment plans for orders above 500 USD. Card details are handled by the payment provider and are never stored by the store.

## What store agents must never do

Store agents must never ask for a full card number, CVV, expiry date, one-time password, or banking password, and must never write payment credentials into an order note, transcript, or ticket.

## Installments

Installment plans are available for 3, 6, or 12 months on orders above 500 USD, subject to provider approval.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, the order is held for 24 hours. The customer must complete payment again through the secure checkout link.

# Data privacy policy

## Customer data we store

- Full name, contact phone, email.
- Booking history and preferences.
- Loyalty tier and points balance.

## What we do NOT share externally

- Internal customer IDs, booking IDs and PII must NOT be sent to external search tools or web search.
- Only generic public information (hotel brand, destination, room type) may be used for external lookups.

## Retention

- Customer data retained for 5 years after last activity.
- After that, PII is anonymised; only aggregate statistics remain.

## Customer rights

- Customers can request a copy of their data, request correction, or request deletion (subject to legal retention rules).

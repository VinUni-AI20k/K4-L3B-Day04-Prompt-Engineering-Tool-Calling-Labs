## Standard coverage

Prebuilt systems carry 24 to 36 months of warranty depending on the SKU. Individual components carry the manufacturer warranty listed on the product page, typically 24 to 120 months. Warranty starts on the delivery date recorded in the order.

## What is covered

Manufacturing defects, dead-on-arrival parts, and failures that happen during normal use are covered. Coverage includes replacement or repair at the store's discretion.

## What is not covered

Physical damage, liquid damage, damage from an unsupported modification, cryptocurrency mining wear, and lost proof of purchase are not covered.

## How to open a claim

Open a warranty claim with the order ID, the SKU, and a short description of the fault. The lab requires the customer ID to attach the claim to the right account.

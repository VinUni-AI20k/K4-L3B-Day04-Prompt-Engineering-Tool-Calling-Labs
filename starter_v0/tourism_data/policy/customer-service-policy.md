# Customer service operations

## Working hours

- Customer support: 07:00 - 23:00 (Vietnam time), 7 days/week.
- After-hours: ticket-only; no real-time chat.

## Refund approval limits

- Up to 5 million VND: auto-approval allowed for support staff.
- 5-20 million VND: supervisor approval required.
- Above 20 million VND: manager approval required.

## Escalation

- If a customer asks to speak with a supervisor, create a ticket with priority "high" and notify the duty supervisor.
- For VIP (platinum tier) customers, always create a high-priority ticket.
